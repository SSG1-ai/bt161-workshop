Overview

Before reviewing any individual agent, your AI Governance Center of Excellence has just come out of a committee meeting with a policy decision that needs to be documented. In this exercise you will create an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) to formalize that policy, then orient yourself in the dashboard and inventory to understand the current state of AI at GlobalFlex AG.

Background

GlobalFlex AG's AI governance committee has concluded that all finance agents interacting with SAP S/4HANA data must run on the Joule Studio runtime and must undergo formal governance review before going live. Your job is to document this as an official Architecture Decision Record so that every agent review from this point forward can be checked against it.

You will use the EA Assistant — the AI-powered assistant in SAP LeanIX — to draft the ADR from a plain-language description.

What is an Architecture Decision Record? An ADR is a short document that captures an important architectural decision: what was decided, why, and what the implications are. Enterprise architects create ADRs when a policy or technical constraint must be made explicit and traceable — so that future reviews can verify compliance. The ADR you create here will be referenced again when you link it to your agent in Exercise 3, turning a committee decision into a verifiable governance control.
1

Create an Architecture Decision

  1. Click the EA Assistant (stars icon) in the header toolbar, beside the Full View switcher.
  2. Enter the prompt below, replacing [PXX] with your participant number.
  3. Review the generated ADR with its Context, Decision, and Consequences sections.
Prompt to paste (replace [PXX])Create an architecture decision titled 'Finance Agents on SAP S/4HANA Must Use Joule Studio Runtime — [PXX]' documenting the following policy: All AI agents that interact with SAP S/4HANA financial data at GlobalFlex AG must be deployed on the Joule Studio runtime. This decision applies to standard and custom-built agents. Agents that do not comply must be migrated or decommissioned. The rationale is auditability, data residency compliance, and alignment with the SAP AI governance framework.
Note The ADR is saved automatically and its status is set to Accepted.
Why your participant number matters Because everyone in this session shares one workspace, including your participant number in the ADR title makes it easy to find your own ADR later when you link it to your agent in Exercise 3.
Why this comes first Architecture decisions document policy at the organization level — before you look at any individual agent. In Exercise 3, you will link your agent to this ADR to show it is subject to this policy.
2

Review the Dashboard

  1. Navigate to the AI Agent Hub Dashboard.
  2. Review the overview, which shows:
    • Total agents, MCP servers, and LLMs.
    • The AI pipeline — Discovery → Inventory → Verified status counts.
    • Agents by verification status.
    • Connected systems (Joule Studio, SAP Cloud ALM, Signavio, and others).
What you are seeing GlobalFlex AG has already connected its SAP system to the AI Agent Hub. The dashboard gives you a real-time governance posture — how many agents exist, how many are verified, and the current level of AI activity across your landscape.
3

Drill into a pre-verified agent

  1. Navigate to the Inventory view.
  2. Filter by Application type + AI Agent subtype.
  3. Open the Billing Adjustment Agent — a pre-loaded, pre-verified standard SAP agent.
  4. Explore the fact sheet tabs:
    • Relations Explorer — how the agent connects to other fact sheets.
    • Analytics — sessions, first/last active.
    • Verification status.
    • Subscriptions — Responsible and Observer.
    • Last Updated — the change log, i.e. the audit trail of every governance action.
This is your audit trail Every field change, subscription update, and verification decision is logged in the Last Updated tab. By the end of the day's exercises this becomes a complete governance record for the agent.
4

Link the ADR to the standard agent

  1. Return to the EA Assistant icon in the header toolbar and use the same active session.
  2. Enter the prompt below.
  3. Confirm the agent now appears in the Affected Fact Sheets section of the ADR — this shows the policy applies retroactively.
Prompt to pastePlease link this architecture decision to the Billing Adjustment Agent fact sheet.
Why this matters Connecting an existing standard agent to the ADR shows that the policy applies retroactively — it covers the entire governed landscape, not only new custom agents.

Summary

You have documented a governance policy as an ADR, seen what a fully governed agent looks like, and linked the standard agent to the policy. In Exercise 2, you will find the custom agent assigned to you in the Discovery Inbox and begin the review process.
