Overview

The agent exists in the inventory but its governance record is incomplete. In this exercise you fill in the fields the EA team can handle independently: assigning yourself as Observer and linking the agent to the Architecture Decision Record from Exercise 1. The owner-dependent fields (AI Risk, AI Model, Business Criticality, Functional Fit, AI Agent Business Value, Technical Fit) are deferred to a survey in Exercise 4.

  • Work through each field below and check its current state.

2

Assign subscribers

  1. Navigate to the Subscriptions section.
  2. Add yourself as an Observer subscriber.
  3. Assign Joanne Rhode (joanne.rhode@sap.com) as the Responsible subscriber — the business owner for this scenario.
What subscriptions do Subscriptions establish accountability and drive update notifications. The Responsible role is the business owner or deploying team — accountable for the agent and the recipient of governance surveys. The Observer is the EA team member handling governance oversight — notified of changes without taking on operational accountability.
3

Validate the description

  1. Read the description carefully.
  2. Confirm it reflects the agent's actual function at GlobalFlex AG — not a generic template.
  3. If it is thin or inaccurate, use LeanIX's built-in AI-generate option (the sparkle icon) in edit mode to improve it.
4

Link the Architecture Decision

  1. Click the EA Assistant icon (stars icon) in the header toolbar (or open the ADR directly from Architecture Decisions).
  2. Find the ADR you created in Exercise 1 — search for your participant number.
  3. Open the ADR and navigate to the Impacted Fact Sheets section.
  4. Add your agent's fact sheet to the ADR.
Why from the ADR side? Architecture decisions are linked by adding fact sheets to the ADR — not by editing individual fact sheets. This keeps the decision as the single source of truth: all agents subject to a policy are listed in one place.
  1. Return to your agent's fact sheet and confirm the ADR now appears in the Decisions panel in the right sidebar.

Summary

The EA team's portion of the governance record is now complete: you are assigned as Observer, the description is confirmed, and the ADR has been linked. The remaining fields — AI Risk, AI Model, Business Criticality, Functional Fit, AI Agent Business Value, and Technical Fit — require input from the agent's business owner. In Exercise 4, you will send them a survey to collect that information.
