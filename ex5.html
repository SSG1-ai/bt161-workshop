Verify the Agent

Complete the governance checklist, set the lifecycle to Active, mark the agent as Verified, and set the Quality Seal.

Home Ex 0 Ex 1 Ex 2 Ex 3 Ex 4 Ex 5 Ex 6

Overview

Verification is a governance decision point. When you mark an agent as verified in SAP LeanIX, you are formally approving it for production use in your organization.

Before you start Confirm the Exercise 4 survey responses are submitted. The fact sheet must show values for AI Risk, Business Criticality, Functional Fit, AI Agent Business Value, and Technical Fit before you proceed.
1

Confirm the governance checklist

Open your agent's fact sheet and verify that every item below is complete:

  • Description — confirmed in Exercise 3
  • Responsible — auto-assigned from deployment (Exercise 2); you are Observer (Exercise 3)
  • Architecture Decision — linked in Exercise 1 and Exercise 3
  • AI Risk — received via survey (Exercise 4)
  • Business Criticality — received via survey (Exercise 4)
  • Functional Fit — received via survey (Exercise 4)
  • AI Agent Business Value — received via survey (Exercise 4)
  • Technical Fit — received via survey (Exercise 4)
  • Lifecycle — set to Active in Step 2 below

If any item is still missing, address it before clicking Verify.

2

Set the lifecycle to Active

  1. Open the fact sheet and find the Lifecycle field in the header "About" section (it shows empty if unset).
  2. Click the field to open the lifecycle editor, which shows the five phases, each with a date field.
  3. In the Active row, set the date to today; leave the other phases empty.
  4. Click Save — the header then shows the current phase as Active.
PhaseMeaning
PlanBeing considered or designed — not built yet.
Phase InBeing built, acquired, or rolled out.
ActiveProductive and in active use.
Phase OutBeing retired or replaced, but still partly in use.
End of LifeNo longer in use (you will set this in Exercise 6).
Date-driven The lifecycle has five phases — Plan, Phase In, Active, Phase Out, End of Life. The system derives the current phase by comparing the dates you set to today, so the current phase (Active) is derived from the date you just set.
Why this matters Marking the agent Active shows it is productive and approved — exactly what you are about to confirm with verification. It also ensures the agent displays correctly on lifecycle and roadmap reports, and it opens the lifecycle you will formally close in Exercise 6.
3

Mark as Verified

  1. Locate the Verification Badge at the top of the fact sheet header.
  2. Click the badge and select Mark as Verified.
  3. A confirmation dialog appears showing governance details (Autonomy Level, Build Type, AI Risk, and Responsibles).
  4. Click Verify AI Agent to confirm — the badge updates to Agent Verified.
4

Set the Quality Seal

  • The Verification Badge is AI-specific — it approves productive use of AI agents, MCP servers, and LLMs.
  • The Quality Seal (Approval State) applies to all fact sheets and signals that data-quality standards are met.
  1. Locate the Quality Seal in the header — a status indicator showing Draft or Check Needed.
  2. Click it and set the state to Approved.
Two distinct signals The Verification Badge is specific to AI agents, MCP servers, and LLMs — it means the agent has been reviewed and approved to run productively (the AI governance signal). The Quality Seal (Approval State) applies to all fact sheets — it signals the data has been reviewed and meets your organization's data-quality standard. Both are meaningful and both should be set.

Summary

You have completed the full AI governance lifecycle for a newly discovered agent: discovery, inventory, governance profile, data collection from the business owner, and formal verification.

Your agent is now verified. This means that from a governance perspective, you have confirmed this agent is approved to be used within your organization — and end users can interact with it for the tasks it was designed for. The full governance record — who reviewed it, what policies apply, what the business owner confirmed — is captured in SAP LeanIX as an auditable history.

Good to know Administrators can configure a custom governance checklist under Administration → AI Governance (viewable but not editable in the shared workshop workspace).
